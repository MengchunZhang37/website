I am completing an M.S. in Biostatistics at the University of Pittsburgh. At Carnegie Mellon University, I work with Bhiksha Raj on speaker verification and serve as a teaching assistant for Introduction to Deep Learning.

My research is in representation learning for speech and physiological signals. I like tracing a result back to the computation that produced it, and using that understanding to guide model design. Research

Convolutional Dynamic Rotary Positional Encoding

Euijin Hong, Meng-Chun Zhang
Interspeech 2026

CD-RoPE adapts positional encoding to local acoustic context in speech recognition. I contributed to method development, robustness evaluation, and ablation studies. Our Branchformer model achieved lower word error rates than RelPos on LibriSpeech with 2.2 million fewer parameters.

Paper

MultiDiffNet: Cross-Subject EEG Decoding

Meng-Chun Zhang*, Kateryna Shapovalenko*, Yucheng Shao, Eddie Guo, Parusha Pradhan
NeurIPS 2025 Workshop BrainBodyFM; AAAI 2026 Workshop Neuro for AI & AI for Neuro

With guidance from Bhiksha Raj, I co-led this work with Kateryna Shapovalenko, designing the model and investigating its latent representations and objectives across four EEG tasks. Unseen-subject accuracy on 26-class SSVEP was 84.72%, versus 81.08% for EEGNet under the same protocol.
